Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026. Contact: support@queuetoken.local.

What we collect

When a customer joins a queue, we store the name they type and the queue number they receive. When an owner creates a queue, we store the queue name and a secret owner key. We do not ask for phone numbers, email addresses, or payment details.

How it is used

The name is shown only to the queue owner, so they know whom they are calling. Other customers see queue numbers only. We use this data only to run the queue.

Storage on your device

Your browser keeps your queue number, the last queue status, and (for owners) the owner key in its local storage, so the app works when your connection drops. We do not use cookies, advertising, or analytics.

How long we keep it

A customer name is erased when that customer is marked as served. A queue and all its data are deleted after 7 days without activity.

Who can see it

We do not sell or share this data. Standard hosting providers process requests on our behalf and keep server logs.

Your rights

To ask us to delete your data or to ask questions about it, write to support@queuetoken.local.
